**Description** [App Store 4000, Play 4000]

Curvelet is a graphing calculator, a symbolic algebra system, a geometry board and a worksheet maker in one app. It works without a connection and needs no account.

Graph
- Type y = x², r = 1 + cos θ, a parametric curve or an inequality, and it is drawn as you type
- Sliders appear for any value you name, with your own range, step and speed
- Tables of points with linear, quadratic, exponential and logarithmic fits
- Any colour, dashed or dotted lines, a polar grid and axis numbers in multiples of π
- Tap a curve for its roots, extrema and intercepts

Exact math
- Derivatives, integrals, limits, sums, factoring and solving, with exact answers such as √2/2 and 1/3
- Step-by-step working for derivatives, equations and integrals
- Matrices, complex numbers, statistics and number theory

Geometry
- Points, segments, circles, polygons, midpoints, perpendiculars, bisectors, intersections, reflections and rotations
- Drag a point and everything built on it follows
- Draw a rough circle or triangle with your finger and it is straightened for you

Calculator
- A large display with exact and decimal answers, a history tape and ans

For class
- Practice problems that check your answer
- Worksheets with figures, ready to print
- Exam-style figures exported as PNG, SVG, PDF or TikZ

Input
- A math keyboard with fractions, roots, integrals and matrices
- Write math by hand or take a photo of it; it is read on the device

Private by design
Your work stays on your device. Nothing you type, draw or photograph is uploaded, and the app collects no data. A share link carries your graph inside the link itself.

Available in English and Korean.

**What's new** (first release)
First release: graphing, symbolic math, geometry, a scientific calculator and worksheets, all offline.

**Category**
Education (secondary: Productivity)

**Age rating**
4+ / Everyone. No ads, no purchases, no user-generated content shared with others, no web browsing.

## Before submitting

- **Name:** check that "Curvelet" is free in each store and as a trademark (KIPRIS, USPTO) before the first upload
- **Bundle IDs:** `com.curvelet.app` (iOS and Android) and `com.curvelet.desktop` (desktop). They can't change after the first upload, so they should sit under a domain you own (curvelet.com); if you use another domain, change them first
- **Accounts:** Apple Developer Program (yearly fee), Google Play Console (one-time fee), Microsoft Partner Center
- **Privacy policy URL:** publish [privacy.md](privacy.md) on the web and link it in each store
- **Support URL or e-mail** for each listing
- **Signing:** an Apple distribution certificate and provisioning profile; an Android upload key; a Windows code-signing certificate if you ship outside the Microsoft Store
